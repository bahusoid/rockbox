# Hiby charge limit placeholder

The option is shown under General Settings -> System -> Limits on Hiby R1 and Hiby R3 Pro II builds.

## Supported values

- Off: Leaves original firmware settings alone, needs reboot to take effect.
- 4.4V (100%+): OF default. Pushes the battery to its maximum charge limit. This is the default for Hiby devices, but it is not recommended for long-term battery health.
- 4.35V (~95 - 100%): The standard full-charge limit for a 3.85V nominal LiHV battery.
- 4.20V (~75% – 85%): A great limit to choose if your goal is to significantly prolong the lifespan and health of the cell.
- 4.10V (~60% – 70%): A conservative limit, often used for long-term storage.
